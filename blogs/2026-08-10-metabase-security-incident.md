On 3 August 2026, an attacker exploited a zero-day vulnerability in Metabase, the third-party analytics tool we use internally, and gained read access to a database holding a copy of Checkly operational data. The attacker bypassed authentication and obtained an administrator session on our Metabase Cloud instance. Metabase has since blocked the attack, patched the vulnerability, and published a security update.
